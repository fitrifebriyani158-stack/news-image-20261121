Entry for make.fun's contest **Sum up 21 November's world news in one image**.

`entry.png` (1024x1024, 29 KB) sums up 21 November 2026's world news in eight story
cards: Russia's missile wave over Ukraine and Poland, the ICJ Rohingya genocide ruling,
the Trump-Xi meeting and nuclear talks, the AI stock rout, BepiColombo reaching Mercury,
Iran downing a US drone, South Africa's local elections, and the Peru earthquake.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the image to `/output/entry.png`.

Run: `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py`
